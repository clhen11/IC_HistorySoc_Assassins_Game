FAQs
How can I find my target?
Be creative and use your tools! You can try Celcat, LinkedIn, Instagram and any other tool you can think of to find a time and place where you can get to your Target!
What are permissible weapons?
Any item labelled as a weapon, stationery, wooden/plastic utensils, nerf/water guns, model weapons that do not actually look like weapons such as an origami gun, etc. Please do not use anything that could be mistaken for a real weapon, as somebody may call the police on you.
What if I want to leave the game?
If you decide you no longer want to be part of the game, just email the umpires at history.society@imperial.ac.uk and we’ll remove you.
I have a problem/question/suggestion regarding the Game, who should I reach out to?
Please email history.society@imperial.ac.uk.
Can I kill my hunters?
No, this is to promote stealth tactics.
Are my targets hunting me?
No, the alliances are hunting each other in a loop.
How do I report a kill?
Both you and the victim must each fill in the appropriate form linked on the homepage. If only one party fills in the form, we will contact both parties to confirm the kill, but this will cause a delay (and extra work for us), so please ensure both of you fill the correct form in. You may also submit photos and further information about the kill.
I just died, is it over for me?
If you have been killed for the first time, you are a Shade, and can earn your way back into the land of the living as an Outcast by killing 2 Outcasts. Please read the Pocket Rules for full detailed information. If you die again, it's really over for you.
